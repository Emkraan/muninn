# Donate
## Why to donate
We work on Muninn in our free time.
Muninn has become a big part of the things we work on - and therefore we invest a lot of time into maintaining Muninn,
providing support and implementing new features.
We also incur monetary costs while developing Muninn for the tools that we need.
A few examples include:
- Renting cloud infrastructure for our services (status page, monitoring, email, analytics, ...)
- Paying for (IDE-) licenses

Using a donation, you help us to cover these expenses and enable the development of Muninn.
Muninn is completely free to use. We'd highly appreciate if you consider donating, especially if you use Muninn in a commercial environment.

## Where to donate
We accept donations through the following channels:
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/emkraan)
- [PayPal](https://www.paypal.com/ncp/payment/Z5LS6SWMFQGU4)

You may also sponsor us directly by providing a useful service / product to us by other means.
You can get into contact with us [here](/docs/community/get-in-touch).
