We want to make Muninn accessible for everyone.
But to do that, we need your help.
Translations are contributed directly through our
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/Emkraan/muninn) - no dedicated translation platform required.

## How to translate

Translation files live in the repository. To add or improve a translation:

1. Fork the [Muninn repository](https://github.com/Emkraan/muninn) on GitHub.
2. Locate the translation files and add or update the strings for your language.
3. Open a pull request with your changes.

A maintainer will review your changes. After a translation has been approved, it will be
merged and included in the next release of Muninn.

If you are unsure how to get started, open an issue on
[GitHub](https://github.com/Emkraan/muninn/issues/new/choose) and we'll help you out.

## Guidelines
1. Translate with context: Some translations can't be translated 1:1 - they need context. Ensure that your translations make sense in the context.
2. Keep things simple and original - no additional characters are needed.
3. Don't translate the same thing twice - if you see a translation that is already translated, don't translate it again unless you have a better translation.
5. Keep your translations as close to the original as possible.
